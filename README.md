**DEEPROOT** — grow the last seed to the Deep Spring. You are the taproot
of the last seed on a drying earth. Hold the mouse and the glowing tip
grows toward your cursor, drinking water to stay alive, steering around
rocks, while grubs crawl up your roots and eat them — click a grub to
squash it. Split the tip to chase two pockets at once, and every 100 m
deeper, pick one boon: a deeper flask, swifter sap, bitter grub-bane sap,
rain sense, stone softener, or a twin sprout. Reach the Deep Spring at
500 m before your energy runs dry, and the seed blooms above the soil.

Five soil layers, each harder than the last: more rocks, faster grubs,
scarcer water, costlier growth. One core mechanic — steer the growing
tip — with real choices: where to grow, when to branch, which boon bends
the run, which pocket is worth the detour.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, just
static files under `game/`. Hold the mouse (or arrow keys) to grow, SPACE
to branch, click grubs to squash them, click a root tip to switch to it,
P to pause, M to mute. All art is drawn in code and all sound is
synthesized with WebAudio.
